app.idl.pro · Off-season · Round 2 concepts

Between
seasons.
Three ways in.

The voting page at app.idl.pro becomes the next-season ticket waitlist — same URL, same one-field capture (email → CTA → done). The brief: make it feel like IDL has entered the space between seasons, not like a temporary page.

Each concept is a different idea, not a different colourway. All three run on the same form state machine and the real IDL kit.

  • 01Real assets only — fonts & checkered video from app.idl.pro; photography, team marks, flags and skylines from idl.pro’s own CDN.
  • 02Real facts only — 0.43-point final, Shrine Expo Hall, five sold-out events, Brotherhood’s undefeated regular season. No invented dates, counters or queue positions.
  • 03Form visible in the first viewport at 390 and 375 wide; 60px thumb-height CTA; every state designed (focus, invalid, loading, success, network error).

What the waitlist
research says.

  • Lead with access, not updates. Pro-team priority lists and season-ticket waitlists (NBA, MLB, WNBA, football clubs) sell “first chance to buy”, never a newsletter. All three CTAs say access: list / early access / pass.
  • One field, no deposit. Many league waitlists ask for a deposit or account. IDL’s advantage is zero friction — so “Free · Email only” is stated, not implied.
  • Say exactly what happens next. Every success state names the email and the promise: first word when tickets go live, ahead of the public on-sale.
  • Honest urgency. Demand proof beats countdowns: five sold-out events and tiered pricing (both from IDL’s own pages). No fake timers or “#1,204 in line”.

Production
notes.

  • Submit is a stub. IDLWaitlist.submit(email) in shared/waitlist.js resolves after 1.3s. Wire it to the voting-app capture endpoint once a direction is picked. Add ?fail to any URL to see the network-error state.
  • Consent keeps the existing Terms & Privacy pattern, linked to idl.pro.
  • Accessibility: labelled inputs, aria-live status, aria-invalid on error, focus moved to the success message, prefers-reduced-motion removes tilt/marquee/intro.
  • Performance: no libraries; one rAF loop per page; 3D and parallax only on fine pointers.
  • Open decision: next-season dates/cities are unannounced, so every page says “next season” / “Season 02 · TBA” rather than a year.
